# English headword facts

The `tem4-open` membership list extracts only normalized English headwords from
`RealKai42/qwerty-learner-vscode` at commit
`e0dc7a09a5e0946d77b2c279f72a89daf3659b97`.

The repository's **code** is MIT licensed. Its README identifies kajweb/Youdao as
the source of dictionary data; no separate licence for that third-party dictionary
content has been established. The data is therefore recorded as `NOASSERTION`,
not MIT. No Chinese translations, definitions, phonetic transcriptions, examples,
recordings or other expressive dictionary material from that file are included.

`LicenseRef-Headword-Facts` identifies this use of unadorned word-membership facts;
it does not purport to grant rights to the underlying dictionary data. The imported
definitions remain in the separately pinned ECDICT pack, under its existing
notices and documented limitations. The source hash, raw file size and complete
upstream chain are retained in `exam-books.sources.json` for review.

OpenEtymology and 2ndLA lists remain separate CC BY-SA 4.0 adaptations. This notice
does not change their licence or the MIT licence of NightStudy application code.
